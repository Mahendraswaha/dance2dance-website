# Dance2Dance

> Dance2Dance is a non-profit social organization and movement studio based in Oslo, Norway. Backed by the technical expertise of Porto Alegre Cia de Dança (Brazil), it delivers world-class workshops in dance, body awareness, and somatic practices.

## Core Pillars
- **Be the Dance**: Workshops for all levels that integrate body awareness, meditation, and dance. Available for individuals, groups, and corporate teams. Focuses on discovering the dance that already exists in you.
- **Biostretch**: A somatic exercise system that reconnects you with your body through movement awareness. Inspired by decades of research in dance, somatic education, and meditation.
- **Kroppsskole**: Body School - an umbrella for diverse practices exploring movement, touch, breath, expression, and presence with guest facilitators.

## Social Impact
Dance2Dance operates on a solidarity model. Workshops priced at market value for external audiences fund 100% scholarships for residents of priority neighborhoods like Tøyen and Grønland in Oslo, providing high-quality cultural access and promoting social inclusion, mastery (mestring), and belonging.

## Special Projects
- **Be the Dance Ung**: A youth program creating a safe space for teenagers in Tøyen/Grønland through movement and artistic expression.
- **Dance2Dance Kvinne**: A social meeting point (møteplass) for women in Tøyen/Grønland focused on physical well-being, empowerment, and sensory reconnection.
